About FixItFast

FixItFast is a free guide site for everyday tech problems: slow Wi-Fi, full phones, laptops that will not start, and apps that keep crashing.

Why this site exists

Most tech help online is long, confusing or full of jargon. We write short, numbered steps that you can follow in a few minutes, in plain language.

How we write our guides

Each guide describes a real problem, explains the likely cause, and gives the fixes in the order that is quickest to try. We update guides when phones and software change.
