FIXITFAST - MY EARNING PLAN (read me first)
===========================================

WHAT THIS SITE IS
Topic: simple fixes for everyday phone, Wi-Fi and PC problems.
Why: people search these problems every day, so traffic keeps coming.
Earning: Google AdSense ads (later: affiliate links, or selling the site).
Honest note: income takes months. Most sites see little for 3-6 months.

FILES (keep all in ONE folder)
index.html    home page
article.html  shows one article
style.css     design
articles.js   ALL 30 ARTICLES (already in, dated 23 Oct - 21 Nov 2026)
about.html, privacy.html, contact.html   needed for AdSense
sitemap.xml, robots.txt   for Google Search Console
FixItFast_30_Day_Schedule.xlsx   my tracking sheet (edit text, add own experience)

STEP 1 - TEST ON MY PC (day 1)
1. Open the folder in VS Code.
2. Right-click index.html > Open with Live Server (or just double-click).
3. Change your@email.com in index.html.

STEP 2 - PUT IT ONLINE FREE (day 1-2)
1. Make a free account on Netlify or Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.
2. Drag and drop the folder. You get a link.
3. Later buy a domain (about $10/year), e.g. fixitfast-guides.com.

STEP 3 - ADD MY 30 ARTICLES (the 30-day schedule)
1. Open articles.js, copy the TEMPLATE block, paste it, edit it.
2. Set "date" to the day it should appear (one per day).
3. Add all 30 in one sitting. The site shows each one automatically
   on its date. Re-upload the folder only when I add new articles.
4. Each article: 600-1000 words, own words, numbered steps, real solutions.
   Do NOT copy from other sites (Google and AdSense reject copied content).

STEP 4 - GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (week 1)
1. Go to search.google.com/search-console, add my site link.
2. Verify ownership (it gives a code or file, follow the steps).
3. Make a sitemap.xml (list my pages) and submit it.
4. Check weekly: which searches bring visitors.

STEP 5 - ADSENSE (after about 20-30 good articles)
1. Needs: About, Privacy Policy, Contact page, original articles.
2. Apply at adsense.google.com, paste the code in the "Ad space" spots.
